FRUIT SALAD

A delicious and healthy recipe!

Welcome to my recipe!

Today we are going to prepare a delicious and fresh fruit salad. It is easy to make and very tasty!


Ingredients

These are the fruits we need:

Banana

1 banana

Apple

1 apple

Strawberries

1 cup

Grapes

1 cup

Mango

1 mango

Melon

1 melon

Papaya

1 Papaya

Kiwi

1 kiwi

Yogurt

1 cup

Optional Ingredients

You can also add:

cheese

Ice Cream

Preparation

  1. First, wash all the fruits carefully.
  2. Then, peel the banana, mango, papaya and kiwi.
  3. Next, cut the banana, apple, mango, melon, papaya and kiwi into small pieces.
  4. After that, cut the strawberries in half and add the grapes.
  5. Then, put all the fruits in a large bowl.
  6. Next, add the yogurt and mix everything carefully.
  7. If you like, add some cheese and ice cream.
  8. Finally, serve the fruit salad and enjoy!

Final Result

Our delicious fruit salad is ready! It is colorful, fresh and tasty.
